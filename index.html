Good questions.

CoachLine is a platform in development that lets fitness creators offer an AI companion based on their coaching style and approved content. The goal is to help with workouts, everyday food questions, and check-ins through messaging.

The planned experience lives in the messaging apps clients already use, with onboarding inside the conversation. Messages and WhatsApp integrations are part of the roadmap. This website currently shows an interactive preview.

The idea is to use the tone, knowledge, and examples you provide, so the companion reflects your approach. It will identify itself as your AI assistant. Voice notes are a future feature and would require your permission to use your voice.

Our proposed model lets creators set their own monthly subscription price, with 80% going to the creator and 20% to CoachLine before applicable fees and expenses. Pricing is still being finalized. Try the earnings calculator to explore your numbers.
